# Summit Quiz Legal

Public, dependency-free Arabic and English legal and support pages for Summit Quiz.

## Routes

- `/` — legal and support hub
- `/privacy/` — privacy policy
- `/terms/` — terms of use
- `/support/` — support instructions and contact

## Local verification

```sh
npm test
```

The site intentionally loads no analytics, advertisements, remote executable scripts, third-party fonts, or consent tags.

The documents describe the current product design and publisher practices; they are not a claim of jurisdiction-specific legal approval.
